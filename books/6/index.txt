## Chapter 1: The Signal

Twelve-year-old Amir was doing a school project on radio telescopes when he picked up something impossible. A pattern in the cosmic noise. A pattern that was intentional. A pattern that was a message.

The message was simple: "We are here. We are waiting. Are you ready to join us?"

Amir recorded it and tried to tell his teachers, but nobody believed him. The telescope was defective, they said. The signal was just natural interference. But Amir knew better. He'd heard intelligence in that message.

He decided to answer.
