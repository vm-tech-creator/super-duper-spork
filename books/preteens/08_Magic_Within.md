# Magic Within
## A Preteen Fantasy Discovery

---

## Chapter 1: The Awakening

Fourteen-year-old Devon thought he was going insane when things started happening around him. When he got angry, lights flickered. When he felt sad, flowers nearby would wilt. When he felt joy, people around him suddenly seemed happier.

His parents took him to doctors. The doctors found nothing wrong. Devon started to believe there was something fundamentally broken about him.

Then Mrs. Chen, the new substitute teacher, pulled him aside after class.

"You have magic," she said simply.

## Chapter 21: The Balanced Path

Devon learned that magic wasn't about power or dominance. It was about balance, responsibility, and understanding the interconnection between all things.

He became a counselor and teacher, helping other young people discover and master their own innate abilities while maintaining ethical boundaries.
