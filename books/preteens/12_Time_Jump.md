# Time Jump
## A Preteen Time Travel Adventure

---

## Chapter 1: The Discovery

Fourteen-year-old Priya discovers a time machine in her grandmother's basement. Her grandmother has been traveling through history, collecting artifacts and changing small events.

When her grandmother disappears, Priya must use the machine to find her.
