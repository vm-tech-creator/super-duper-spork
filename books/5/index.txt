## A Preteen Survival Adventure

## Chapter 1: Stranded

Twelve-year-old Marcus thought the hiking trip was going to be boring. He didn't count on getting separated from his scout troop during a sudden storm.

One moment he was following the group along the mountain trail, the next moment heavy rain obscured everything, and he was running blindly, crashing through brush. By the time the rain cleared, the trail was gone. His group was gone. And his phone had no signal.

Marcus took a deep breath. Panic wouldn't help. The scout manual had a chapter on this—what to do if you're lost in the wilderness. 

First: stay calm. Check. Second: find shelter. Third: make a signal. Fourth: wait for rescue.

He found a cave about a quarter mile away and settled in as darkness fell.

## Chapter 21: The New Scout

When Marcus was brought back to civilization, he had changed. He understood what he was capable of. He understood that survival wasn't about absence of fear—it was about acting despite fear.

He earned his wilderness survival merit badge, and more importantly, he realized he had a gift for staying calm under pressure.

He went on to become the scout troop's most respected member and eventually a wilderness guide, helping others learn to respect and navigate the natural world.
