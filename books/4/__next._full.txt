## Chapter 1: The Message in a Bottle

Zara found the bottle wedged between rocks on the beach, half-buried in sand. It was old, with barnacles crusted on its surface, and inside was a rolled piece of waterproof parchment.

With trembling fingers, she pulled out the message and unrolled it. The handwriting was elegant but urgent:

"To whoever finds this: my people are in danger. The ocean city of Meridia is under threat from those who would destroy it. If you can read this, you have the sight. You can see what others cannot. Please help us. The coordinates are below."

Zara's heart raced. The sight? But she could see things. She'd always been able to. The way currents moved beneath the water's surface. The emotions of the creatures that lived below. The hidden pathways through the reef.

She'd always thought she was just imaginative.

## Chapter 21: The Bridge to Two Worlds

Zara learned to live between two worlds—the surface and the depths. She attended school like a normal girl, but every evening she dove into the ocean to serve as Meridia's ambassador to the surface world, helping to heal the damage humans had done to the ocean and its inhabitants.

She became known as the Ocean's Voice, the girl who could speak for creatures without words, who worked to reduce pollution and protect marine habitats.

And she always carried the bottle with her—a reminder of the message that changed her life.
