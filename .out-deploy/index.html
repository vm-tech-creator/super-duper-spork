Why Sahara

Built to feel fast.

Motion and color support the content—nothing screams for attention unless you ask it to.

Snappy interactions

Hover, focus, and scroll states use short, consistent timing so the UI feels tight, not twitchy.

One coherent shell

Navigation, cards, and CTAs share the same radii and borders—easier to scan, harder to get lost.

Themes that respect you

Switch looks without breaking layout. Classic stays restrained; experimental modes stay optional.

Layered depth

Soft glass, light grain, and restrained shadows add depth instead of flat “template” blocks.

Touch-friendly targets

Buttons and chips are sized for real thumbs—not mouse-only hit areas from 2014.

Delight in the details

Micro-motion on cards and hero elements rewards exploration without slowing anyone down.

About

A supersite for curious people.
