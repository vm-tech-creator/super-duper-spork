# Garden Mystery
## A Preteen Magical Realism

---

## Chapter 1: The Return

Twelve-year-old Emma returns to her grandmother's house after three years away. The garden has grown wild and strange—plants that shouldn't exist are flourishing there.

Her grandmother reveals that the garden is magical and needs Emma's help.
