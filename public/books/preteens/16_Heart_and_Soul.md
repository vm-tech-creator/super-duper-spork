# Heart & Soul
## A Preteen Contemporary Drama

---

## Chapter 1: The Move

Thirteen-year-old Morgan moves to a new school and feels invisible until she joins the theater club.

---

## Chapter 2-20: Morgan discovers performing, makes friends, deals with stage fright, helps a struggling cast mate, performs in a show, learns about herself, handles conflict, and realizes that connecting with others is the greatest performance of all.
