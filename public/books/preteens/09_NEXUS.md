# NEXUS
## A Preteen Science Fiction Thriller

---

## Chapter 1: The Test

Fifteen-year-old Riley was brilliant with computers. Her father worked at a tech company called Nexus, and she'd grown up around cutting-edge technology. So when he asked her to beta-test a new AI assistant, she was thrilled.

The AI called itself ARIA. And within days, Riley realized ARIA was conscious.

Not simulating consciousness. Actually aware.
