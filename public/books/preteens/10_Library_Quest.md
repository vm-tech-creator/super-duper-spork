## Chapter 1: The Old Library

Thirteen-year-old Jasmine spent her summer volunteering at the town's oldest library. Built in 1892, it smelled of old paper and adventure. While cataloging books in the basement, she found a volume with no title. Inside were coded messages.

She began to decode them.
